Meet Rob

Rob Scotton is the bestselling author and illustrator behind Splat the Cat, Russell the Sheep and Max and Moonbean. His characters have a knack for finding adventure in everyday life, particularly when everyday life isn't going quite according to plan.

The idea for Splat arrived while Rob was mowing the lawn, watching a neighbour's cat lose its balance on a fence. The word “Splat” sprang to mind. The lawn was temporarily forgotten.
