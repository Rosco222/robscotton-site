New from Rob Scotton

Max and Moonbean

Max's show‑and‑tells always go wrong. Then one night a shooting star crashes into his closet, and out steps a small blue astronaut who talks in bubbles. His name is Moonbean, and he has a question: will Max come to the moon to be his show‑and‑tell?

A story about a shy pup, a brave one, and the moment you realise they're the same dog.

Look inside Where to buy

Meet the two of them

Max

A pup with a woolly hat, a teddy called Mr Ted and a goldfish. Clever, kind, of impeccable style, and quite sure he is not very brave.

Moonbean

A strange blue thingy with a slightly odd stare who arrived by shooting star. Speaks in bubble‑speak, tells excellent jokes, and says “Piffaffle!” to the word can't.
