Talking about the story

Questions for reading together at home or in class.

  • Max’s show-and-tells always seem to go wrong. Have you ever felt nervous about doing something in front of other people? What helped?
  • When the blue thingy appears, Max wants to hide, but he steps forward and asks its name. What does being brave mean to you?
  • Moonbean calls Max a “pup of courage”. Who helps you feel brave?
  • If a shooting star landed in your room tonight, what would you hope to find?
  • Moonbean speaks in bubble-speak. Could you and a friend invent a secret language of your own?
