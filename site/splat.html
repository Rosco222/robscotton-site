Splat's world

Meet the cast: the friends, the frenemy and the ducks.

Splat

Fur that goes everywhere, a heart to match. Worries big, bounces back bigger.

Seymour

Small, quirky and full of personality. Splat's best friend and the brains of most operations.

Spike

A greyish‑white cat who really wants to be Splat's friend, but keeps breaking his favourite toy and eating all the candy fish.

The ducks

Splat's father makes toy ducks for a living. Splat has given every one of them a name, and counts them. Often.
