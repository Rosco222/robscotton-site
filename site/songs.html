Splat the Cat

His Tail Wiggled On

Splat’s first day at Cat School, with Seymour hiding in his lunchbox. Splat rides, wobbles and wiggles his way through a song of his own. Press play and let his tail do the rest.

His Tail Wiggled OnRob Scotton's Splat the Cat
0:00

Nervous about starting school and making friends, Splat hides his mouse companion Seymour in his lunchbox. Lessons begin well, but Seymour’s escape causes excitement among the other cats. With help from Mrs. Wimpydimple, Splat discovers that school can be welcoming, and that an unexpected friend has something valuable to offer.

Russell the Sheep

What’s a Sheep to Do?

Russell the Sheep can’t get to sleep in Frogsbottom Field. He tries a rusty old car, a tree full of owls and a sky full of stars, until he finds the one sheep he forgot to count.

What’s a Sheep to Do?Rob Scotton's Russell the Sheep
0:00

Night falls on Frogsbottom Field and every sheep is fast asleep, except Russell. He tries everything: a new hat, a bed up a tree, counting his feet, counting the stars. There’s only one thing left to try. Counting sheep!
