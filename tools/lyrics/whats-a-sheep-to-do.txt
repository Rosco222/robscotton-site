[Instrumental Intro: Two bars, piano and clarinet]
[Intro]
Go to sleep, little sheep,
Go to sleep, little sheep.
Close your eyes,
Not one peep.
Go to sleep, little sheep.
[Verse 1]
Under stars in Frogsbottom Field,
The sheep were fast asleep.
Not a stir, not a whisper,
Not a sound, not a peep.
But Russell tossed,
And Russell turned,
And Russell gave a sigh.
His head was all a-whirr,
And he couldn’t tell you why.
[Conversational]
“I can’t sleep!” he bleated.
“Oh, this just isn’t funny!
I’m tired from my nose
To the wool upon my tummy!”
[Chorus]
What’s a sheep to do?
What’s a sheep to do?
When everyone is fast asleep,
And sleep won’t come to you?
What’s a sheep to do?
Please give me a clue!
I only want a little sleep,
Oh, what’s a sheep to do?
[Verse 2]
“Find a place, settle down,
Rest your eyes and lose that frown!”
A rusty car, without a roof,
Looked rather cosy - sheep approved
But the junk in the trunk
Was piled up in a heap.
“No room for Russell!
No room for sheep!”
He wandered off beneath a tree,
Then looked above.
[Spoken softly]
“Aha!” said he.
[Chorus]
What’s a sheep to do?
What’s a sheep to do?
If I can’t sleep down here,
I’ll try a higher view!
[Verse 3]
He found a branch,
A cosy spot,
And tucked himself
Into a knot.
He curled his legs,
He closed his eyes.
[Soft]
“Goodnight, goodnight!”
[Comic anticipation]
But then, a surprise!
[Owls]
“Too-woo! Who You!”
The owls complained.
“Excuse us, sheep!
Have you gone insane?”
“This branch is taken!
Can’t you see?
There’s simply no room
For a sheep in our tree!”
Russell tumbled to the ground
And grumbled at the dreadful sound.
[Chorus]
What’s a sheep to do?
What’s a sheep to do?
I can’t sleep here!
I can’t sleep there!
There’s nothing I can do!
What’s a sheep to do?
Oh, give me a clue!
The whole wide world is fast asleep,
So why can’t I sleep too?
[Verse 4]
“I know!” cried Russell.
“I’ll count the stars!
The little ones,
The bright ones,
The one that looks like Mars!”
He counted high,
He counted low,
He counted fast,
He counted slow.
A hundred stars!
A thousand more!
And still they stretched
For evermore.
His eyes grew tired,
His numbers flew.
[Weary]
“Oh, honestly!
What’s a sheep to do?”
[Chorus]
What’s a sheep to do?
What’s a sheep to do?
I’ve counted half the universe,
And still I can’t sleep too!
[Verse 5]
Then little Frog came hopping by,
And blinked at Russell’s weary eye.
[Frog]
“Why count the stars?
They’re much too high!
Try counting sheep
As they quietly lie!”
[Russell]
“Of course!” said Russell.
“That’s the trick!
I’ll count them all —
And make it quick!”
[Counting]
One … two, three …
Four … five, six, Seven … eight … nine …
[Long pause]
[Soft sigh]
“Still wide awake,”
Poor Russell sighed.
[Chorus]
What’s a sheep to do?
What’s a sheep to do?
I’ve counted every sheep I know,
And still I can’t sleep too.
[Verse 6]
But wait a minute!
Could it be?
There’s one more sheep
I didn’t see!
He’d counted all his friends asleep,
But missed one very special sheep.
Not far away,
Not up a tree,
But closer still —
Who could it be?
A sheep so small,
A sheep so near,
A sheep who’d been there
All along, right here.
[Brief silence]
[Spoken, delighted but quiet]
“That’s me!” said Russell,
With a sleepy grin.
[Almost whispered]
“Ten.”
[Soft piano resolution]
And Russell yawned,
His eyes shut tight,
And drifted off
Into the night.
[Refrain]
What’s a sheep to do?
Now Russell knew.
When you’re counting all the others,
Never forget that you count too.
You count too,
Yes, you count too.
No matter how small you feel,
You count too.
[Outro]
Go to sleep, little sheep,
Go to sleep, little sheep.
Close your eyes,
Not one peep.
Under stars in Frogsbottom Field,
The moon shines softly through
Go to sleep, Russell the Sheep.
[Gentle whisper]
You count too.
[End: Soft piano, natural decay]
